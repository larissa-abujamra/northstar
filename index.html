01 · What we do

Every signal a robot
needs to learn from a human.

  • Egocentric Capture

    First-person video captured from head-mounted cameras during live production work. Synchronized RGB and depth streams - see exactly what a skilled operator sees, with full spatial context.

  • Tactile Data

    Force and pressure signals from instrumented gloves — the contact information nothing else captures.

  • UMI Gripper

    Handheld grippers that let operators record fine manipulation demonstrations — the contact-rich data robots need to learn dexterous, two-handed tasks.
